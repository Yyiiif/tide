Welcome to Reflow
Your spending, your way. No judgements — just clarity on where your money actually flows.
Set your Comfort Zone
Go to Base → Comfort Zone and drag the slider to a number that feels right for this month. Not a hard limit — just your guide.
Manual
AI Scan
Log your drops
Tap the floating button on the Drops tab. Type an expense manually, or scan a payment app screenshot with AI.
See your streams
The Reflow home screen shows each category as a bar. Tap any stream to see every expense inside it.
Track your real balance
Log your bank balance once in Base → Balance. Reflow will track exactly how much you've spent since that snapshot — no bank linking needed.
Big month? Add an event
Travelling, moving, celebrating? Go to Base → Event list to add a special event — and give yourself extra room, automatically.
You're all set
Start with one drop — anything you spent today. The picture will build itself from there.
